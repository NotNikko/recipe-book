title: "Sourdough Berry Muffins"
description: "Delicious, flavorful muffins packed with your choice of berries."
image: "https://brokenovenbaking.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/blueberry-sourdough-discard-muffins-11.jpg"
category: "Baking"
tags:
  - "muffin"
  - "berry"
  - "sourdough"
prepTime: 20
cookTime: 25
servings: 12
difficulty: Easy
featured: false
favorite: false
ingredients:
  - "390g sourdough starter discard"
  - "190g (3/4 cup) unsalted butter, melted and cooled"
  - "85-90g warm milk"
  - "3 whole eggs (or 2 large eggs)"
  - "430g all-purpose flour"
  - "260g sugar (or less, to taste)"
  - "2 tsp baking powder"
  - "1 tsp baking soda"
  - "1 tsp salt"
  - "340g frozen berries of your choice"
instructions:
  - "Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners."
  - "Toss the frozen berries with 1/2 tablespoon of flour to coat — this helps keep them from sinking to the bottom of the muffins."
  - "Whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl."
  - "In a separate bowl, whisk together the sourdough starter, melted butter, warm milk, and eggs until smooth."
  - "Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir just until combined."
  - "Gently fold in the flour-coated berries, being careful not to overmix."
  - "Divide the batter evenly among the 12 muffin cups, filling each about three-quarters full."
  - "Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean."
  - "Cool in the pan for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack."
