description: "Blueberry and lemon mixed scones.

It's  a dry dough."

ingredients:
  - "2 cups (250g) flour"
  - "1/4 or 1/3 cup (50-100g) sugar"
  - "1/2 tsp salt"
  - "2 tsp baking powder"
  - "115g (1 stick) unsalted butter (frozen & grated)"
  - "1 cup blueberry"
  - "1 lemon zest"
  - "1/2 cup (100g) sourdough starter"
  - "1 egg"
  - "2 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "2 tbsp any milk"
instructions:
  - "Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper."
  - "Whisk together the flour, sugar, salt, and baking powder in a large bowl."
  - "Add the frozen, grated butter to the dry ingredients and toss gently to coat, working quickly to keep it cold."
  - "Fold in the blueberries and lemon zest until evenly distributed."
  - "In a separate bowl, whisk together the sourdough starter, egg, vanilla extract, and milk until smooth."
  - "Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir just until a shaggy dough forms — the dough should remain dry and crumbly, not sticky."
  - "Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and knead gently 2 to 3 times, just enough to bring it together."
  - "Pat the dough into an 8-inch round, about 1 inch thick, and cut into 8 wedges."
  - "Arrange the wedges on the prepared baking sheet, spacing them about 2 inches apart."
  - "Bake for 20 to 24 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean."
  - "Cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack."
