ingredients:
  - "Base Layer:"
  - "180g graham crackers (about 1½ cups crushed)"
  - "98g sweetened shredded coconut (about 1 cup)"
  - "65g walnuts (about ½ cup)"
  - "113g unsalted butter (8 tablespoons)"
  - "67g granulated sugar (about ⅓ cup)"
  - "26g unsweetened cocoa powder (5 tablespoons), sifted"
  - "1 large egg, beaten"
  - "Custard Filling:"
  - "84g unsalted butter (6 tablespoons), softened"
  - "173g powdered sugar (about 1½ cups)"
  - "36g custard powder, such as Bird's (3 tablespoons)"
  - "2 tablespoons whole milk"
  - "1 teaspoon vanilla extract"
  - "¼ teaspoon kosher salt"
  - "Chocolate Layer:"
  - "170g semi-sweet or dark chocolate (1 cup), chopped"
  - "42g unsalted butter (3 tablespoons)"
instructions:
  - "Grease an 8- by 8-inch baking pan with nonstick baking spray, or line it with two long strips of parchment paper to cover the bottom and all sides, creasing as needed for a secure fit."
  - "In the bowl of a food processor, pulse together the graham crackers, shredded coconut, and walnuts until finely ground, then transfer the mixture to a medium bowl."
  - "Place the butter in a medium heatproof bowl set over a saucepan of simmering water. Once melted, whisk in the sugar and cocoa powder until dissolved, then add the beaten egg and mix until smooth and slightly thickened, about 2 minutes."
  - "Pour the chocolate-butter mixture over the graham cracker crumbs and toss to coat evenly. Gently knead until cohesive, then press the mixture into the prepared pan in an even layer, compressing firmly with a flat-bottomed glass or measuring cup. Freeze until firm, about 10 minutes."
  - "To make the custard filling, beat together the softened butter, powdered sugar, custard powder, milk, vanilla, and salt with a hand mixer (or stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment) on medium speed until smooth, about 3 minutes. Remove the crust from the freezer and spread the custard filling over it in an even layer using an offset spatula. Refrigerate until set, about 1 hour, then transfer to the freezer until ready to top with chocolate."
  - "For the chocolate layer, combine the chopped chocolate and butter in a medium heatproof bowl set over a saucepan of barely simmering water. Stir frequently until fully melted and glossy, about 5 minutes, then remove from heat."
  - "Remove the pan from the freezer and spread the melted chocolate evenly over the chilled custard layer using an offset spatula. Refrigerate until fully set, about 30 minutes."
  - "To serve, slice into 2-inch bars using a hot knife — run the blade under warm water and dry it between each cut for the cleanest edges. Return any unserved bars to the refrigerator."
