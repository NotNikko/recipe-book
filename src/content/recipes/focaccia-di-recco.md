ingredients:
  - "90g active sourdough starter (or discard)"
  - "450g all-purpose flour"
  - "340g warm water"
  - "10g fine salt"
  - "Olive oil, plus more for the pan and drizzling"
  - "8 oz brie cheese, torn into small dollops"
instructions:
  - "In a large bowl, combine the sourdough starter, warm water, and flour. Mix until no dry flour remains."
  - "Sprinkle in the salt and knead directly in the bowl, or on a lightly oiled counter, for about 5 minutes, until the dough is smooth and elastic."
  - "Drizzle the dough with olive oil, cover, and let it rest for about 10 minutes to relax the gluten — this makes it much easier to stretch paper-thin later."
  - "Meanwhile, preheat your oven as hot as it will go, ideally 500°F (260°C), with a large sheet pan or pizza pan inside to heat up."
  - "Divide the dough into two pieces, one slightly larger than the other. On a well-oiled surface, stretch the larger piece by hand until it's almost see-through and drapes over the edges of your pan."
  - "Carefully transfer the sheet of dough into the hot, oiled pan, letting the excess hang over the sides."
  - "Dot the surface evenly with small dollops of brie, leaving a little space between each piece."
  - "Stretch the second, smaller piece of dough just as thin and lay it over the cheese, pressing it against the bottom layer and pinching the overhanging edges together to seal."
  - "Tear a few small holes across the top layer with your fingers to let steam escape and cheese bubble through, then drizzle generously with olive oil and a pinch of flaky salt."
  - "Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until the top is deeply golden with dark blistered spots."
  - "Slice and serve immediately while hot and the cheese is still molten."
