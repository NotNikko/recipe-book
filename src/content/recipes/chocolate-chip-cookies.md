---
title: "Chocolate Chip Cookies"
description: "Soft, bakery-style cookies with crisp edges and a chewy center — better than anything from a box."
image: "/images/recipes/cookies.jpg"
category: "Baking"
tags:
  - "cookies"
  - "chocolate"
  - "dessert"
prepTime: 15
cookTime: 13
servings: 24
difficulty: Easy
featured: false
favorite: false
ingredients:
  - "2¼ cups all-purpose flour"
  - "1 tsp baking soda"
  - "1 tsp fine salt"
  - "1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened"
  - "¾ cup granulated sugar"
  - "¾ cup packed brown sugar"
  - "2 large eggs, room temperature"
  - "2 tsp pure vanilla extract"
  - "2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips"
instructions:
  - "Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C) and line two baking sheets with parchment paper."
  - "Whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl. Set aside."
  - "Beat the softened butter and both sugars together in a large bowl until light and fluffy, about 3 minutes."
  - "Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Mix in the vanilla extract."
  - "Gradually add the flour mixture, stirring just until combined — don't overmix."
  - "Fold in the chocolate chips with a spatula until evenly distributed."
  - "Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the prepared baking sheets, spacing them 2 inches apart."
  - "Bake for 9 to 11 minutes until the edges are set and golden but the centers still look slightly underdone."
  - "Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack."
